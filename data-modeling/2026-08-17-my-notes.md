## My understanding

### Data Modeling Layers

I understood data modeling as having 3 layers:

1. **Conceptual** — overview, stakeholder requirements, high-level understanding.
2. **Logical** — tables, relationships between tables, ER/crowfoot diagrams, etc.
3. **Physical** — SQL equivalent/implementation of the logical model.

Data modeling is the concept of creating relationships or understanding the overall connection between tables used. It is the heart of a data project as it involves the complete flow.

### OLTP vs OLAP

- **OLTP (Online Transaction Processing)** — transactional modeling. We care about how each transaction is saved, how transactions are stored, and transactional workloads. This is mostly adding/updating records in existing data.
- **OLAP (Online Analytical Processing)** — built specifically for analytical purposes, business dashboards, reporting, and understanding how data flows for analysis.

### Keys

- **Primary key:** unique and non-null.
- **Candidate key:** keys that are candidates to become the primary key. Example: mobile number could be a candidate key in a users table with user_id as the primary key.
- **Alternate key:** forgot.
- **Super key:** forgot.
- **Foreign key:** I understand this through an example: in an Orders and Customers relationship, customer_id is a foreign key in Orders and order_id is the primary key in Orders.
